About The Wisher’s Wish

A free tool to create animated, personalised wish pages and share them with the people you care about, in under a minute.

How it works

📝

1. Fill in the details

Choose the occasion, add both names and write a message, or let us write it for you.

🎨

2. Pick a look

Choose a theme, animation and font. Add a secret code or set the time the wish opens.

🔗

3. Share the link

Send it on WhatsApp, copy the link, or download the QR code.

Why you’ll like it

🆓

Completely free

No payments, no hidden limits. The site is supported by ads.

🚫

No sign-up

No account and no email needed. Just create and share.

🔒

Nothing stored

Your wish lives only inside its link. We never save it on any server.
