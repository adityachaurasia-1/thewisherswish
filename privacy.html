Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to The Wisher’s Wish. Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information is used when you visit this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not create accounts and we do not store personal data on any server. The details of a wish (names, message, theme and similar choices) exist only inside the link that you create and share. Anyone who has the link can see the wish, so please share it only with the people you want.

2. Cookies and advertising

This website may show ads from Google AdSense and other third-party vendors. These vendors may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

3. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites, such as WhatsApp when you share a wish. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

4. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

6. Contact
